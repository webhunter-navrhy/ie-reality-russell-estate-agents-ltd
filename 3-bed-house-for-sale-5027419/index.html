Description

Russell Estate Agents are delighted to present this attractive three-bedroom semi-detached home ideally situated in the popular residential development of Careystown Meadows, just a short stroll from the picturesque coastal village of Whitegate in East Cork. The property offers bright, well-proportioned accommodation arranged over two floors and is ideally suited to first-time buyers, young families or those looking to settle in a well-established residential area close to the coast. It is presented in excellent condition throughout with its current owners having completed a number of upgrades in the house in recent years. The property has the benefit of a B Ber rating ensure it qualifies for the green mortgage. Whitegate enjoys an attractive position on the eastern side of Cork Harbour and offers a good range of local amenities and recreational facilities, while Midleton is approximately 1215 minutes away by car. The wider area is particularly popular for its coastal setting, beaches, walks and sporting amenities.

OUTSIDE

The property benefits from a well-proportioned rear garden, offering an attractive and practical outdoor space. A patio area provides an ideal setting for outdoor dining, entertaining or relaxing during the warmer months, while the adjoining lawn area offers plenty of space for children, pets or gardening enthusiasts. The garden also includes a useful garden shed, providing convenient storage for gardening equipment, bicycles and other outdoor items. Overall, the garden offers a pleasant combination of patio, lawn and practical storage, making it an excellent extension of the home.

LOCATION

Whitegate is a picturesque coastal village in East Cork, beautifully positioned on the eastern shores of Cork Harbour. Combining a rich maritime heritage with a strong sense of community, the village enjoys stunning waterfront views and a peaceful atmosphere, making it an attractive place to live and visit.

Surrounded by scenic countryside and coastline, Whitegate offers easy access to nearby beaches, woodland walks, sailing facilities, and the renowned Trabolgan Holiday Village. The village is also within a short drive of Midleton, providing convenient access to an excellent range of shops, schools, restaurants, and commuter links to Cork City.

Whitegate has a Eurospar supermarket selection of pubs and the well regarded Corkbeg Coffee on the seafront. It has a National School and there is a bus service to all of the Secondary Schools in Midleton also.
